## What is Dayflow?

Dayflow is a **native macOS app** that records your screen at **1 FPS**, analyzes it **every 15 minutes** with AI, and generates a **timeline** of your activities with summaries.

It's lightweight (25MB app size) and uses ~100MB of RAM and <1% CPU.

## Why Dayflow?

Dayflow was built on the principle that your calendar isn't the source of truth for how you actually spend your time—your screen is. It provides a calm, trustworthy timeline that lets you see your workday without turning into yet another dashboard you have to maintain.

**Privacy-minded by design**: You choose your AI provider. Use **Gemini** (bring your own API key), **local models** (Ollama / LM Studio), or **ChatGPT/Claude** (requires paid subscription).
