## Overview

Dayflow automatically tracks your screen activity and generates a timeline with AI-powered summaries. This page covers all features and how to use them.

## Core features

### Automatic timeline

Dayflow records your screen at 1 FPS and analyzes it every 15 minutes to create timeline cards.

<img
  src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JerryZLiu/dayflow/main/docs/images/hero_animation_1080p.gif"
  alt="Dayflow timeline"
  width="800"
/>

**How it works**:
1. Screen captured at 1 FPS in 15-second chunks
2. Every 15 minutes, recent footage is sent to your AI provider
3. AI generates timeline cards with activity summaries
4. Cards appear in your timeline with timestamps and categories

**Timeline cards include**:
- Activity title and description
- Start and end time
- Duration
- Category (work, personal, distractions)
- App icons and context

### Watch timelapses

Review your entire day in fast-forward by watching timelapse videos of your screen activity.

**To watch a timelapse**:
1. Click on any timeline card
2. Click the **Play** button
3. Watch your screen activity for that time period

**Timelapse features**:
- Adjustable playback speed
- Scrub through timeline
- Jump to specific moments
- Full-screen mode

<Tip>
  Timelapses are great for remembering what you worked on or finding when you saw something specific.
</Tip>

### Distraction tracking

Dayflow automatically identifies when you get distracted and highlights it in your timeline.

**What counts as a distraction**:
- Social media (Twitter, Reddit, Instagram, etc.)
- Entertainment sites (YouTube, Netflix, etc.)
- News sites
- Messaging apps (when not work-related)

**Distraction insights**:
- Total distraction time per day
- Most common distractions
- Distraction patterns by time of day
- Distraction-free focus periods

<Note>
  You can customize what counts as a distraction in settings.
</Note>

### Timeline export

Export your timeline as Markdown for any date range.

**To export**:
1. Click the **Export** button in the timeline view
2. Select date range
3. Choose format (Markdown, CSV, or JSON)
4. Save to file

**Export includes**:
- All timeline cards with timestamps
- Activity summaries
- Categories and tags
- Duration for each activity

**Use cases**:
- Weekly reports for managers
- Personal productivity tracking
- Time tracking for clients
- Journaling and reflection

### Auto storage cleanup

Dayflow automatically manages storage to prevent unlimited growth.

**Storage settings**:
- Set limit: 1GB to 20GB, or unlimited
- Auto-cleanup: Removes oldest recordings when limit is reached
- Manual cleanup: Delete recordings older than X days

**Storage locations**:
- Recordings: `~/Library/Application Support/Dayflow/recordings/`
- Database: `~/Library/Application Support/Dayflow/chunks.sqlite`

**To configure**:
1. Open Dayflow settings
2. Go to **Storage** tab
3. Set your preferred limit
4. Enable/disable auto-cleanup

<Warning>
  Deleted recordings cannot be recovered. Timeline cards remain, but you won't be able to watch timelapses for deleted periods.
</Warning>

## Daily journal (beta)

Set intentions, reflect on your day, and get AI-generated summaries of your activity.

  src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JerryZLiu/dayflow/main/docs/images/JournalPreview.png"
  alt="Dayflow journal"

/>

<Note>
  Journal is currently in beta with limited access. Enter your access code in the app to unlock it.

### Morning intentions

Plan what you want to accomplish each day.

**To set intentions**:
1. Open the Journal tab
2. Click **Set Intentions**
3. Write what you want to accomplish today
4. Save

**Features**:
- Free-form text entry
- AI suggestions based on past patterns
- Reminder notifications (configurable)
- Review at end of day

### Evening reflections

Review how your day actually went compared to your intentions.

**To reflect**:
1. Open the Journal tab
2. Click **Reflect on Day**
3. Review your timeline and intentions
4. Write your reflection
5. Save

**Features**:
- Side-by-side view of intentions and actual timeline
- AI-generated summary of your day
- Highlight accomplishments and distractions
- Track patterns over time

### AI summaries

Get automatic summaries of your day based on your timeline.

**Summary includes**:
- Total productive time
- Main activities and projects
- Distraction time and patterns
- Focus periods
- Comparison to intentions

### Scheduled reminders

Set up notifications to remind you to set intentions and reflect.

**To configure**:
1. Open Dayflow settings
2. Go to **Journal** tab
3. Enable reminders
4. Set times for morning and evening notifications

**Reminder options**:
- Morning intentions: 8:00 AM (default)
- Evening reflections: 6:00 PM (default)
- Custom times
- Skip weekends

### Weekly view

See patterns across your week.

**Weekly insights**:
- Total productive time per day
- Distraction trends
- Most productive days
- Focus time patterns
- Intention completion rate

## Recording controls

### Start/stop recording

Control when Dayflow records your screen.

**From menu bar**:
1. Click the Dayflow icon
2. Select **Start Recording** or **Stop Recording**

**From main window**:
- Toggle the recording switch in the top-right corner

**From automation**:
- Use URL schemes: `dayflow://start-recording` or `dayflow://stop-recording`
- See [Automation](/automation) for details

<Tip>
  Stop recording during sensitive activities like banking or entering passwords.
</Tip>

### Pause manager

Automatically pause recording during inactivity.

**To configure**:
1. Open Dayflow settings
2. Go to **Recording** tab
3. Enable **Pause on inactivity**
4. Set inactivity threshold (default: 5 minutes)

**How it works**:
- Detects when you haven't interacted with your Mac
- Automatically pauses recording after threshold
- Resumes when you return

### Active display tracking

Dayflow only records the display you're actively using.

**Multi-monitor support**:
- Automatically detects which display has focus
- Only records the active display
- Switches seamlessly between displays

## Categories

Organize your timeline with custom categories.

### Default categories

- **Work**: Professional activities
- **Personal**: Personal projects and activities
- **Distractions**: Social media, entertainment, etc.

### Custom categories

Create your own categories to organize your timeline.

**To create a category**:
1. Open Dayflow settings
2. Go to **Categories** tab
3. Click **Add Category**
4. Set name, color, and rules
5. Save

**Category rules**:
- App-based: Categorize by application
- URL-based: Categorize by website domain
- Keyword-based: Categorize by window title keywords
- Time-based: Categorize by time of day

### Category editing

Edit timeline cards to change their category.

**To edit**:
1. Click on a timeline card
2. Click the category dropdown
3. Select a new category
4. Changes are saved automatically

## Settings

### General

- **Launch at login**: Start Dayflow automatically when you log in
- **Show in menu bar**: Display Dayflow icon in menu bar
- **Check for updates**: Automatically check for app updates

### Recording

- **Frame rate**: 1 FPS (fixed)
- **Analysis interval**: 15 minutes (fixed)
- **Pause on inactivity**: Auto-pause after X minutes
- **Recording location**: Choose where to store recordings

### Storage

- **Storage limit**: 1GB to 20GB, or unlimited
- **Auto-cleanup**: Remove old recordings automatically
- **Manual cleanup**: Delete recordings older than X days

### AI Provider

- **Provider**: Gemini, Local, ChatGPT, or Claude
- **API key**: For Gemini
- **Endpoint**: For local models
- **Model**: For local models

### Categories

- **Manage categories**: Create, edit, delete categories
- **Category rules**: Set up automatic categorization
- **Default category**: Set default for uncategorized activities

### Journal

- **Enable journal**: Turn journal feature on/off
- **Morning reminder**: Set time for intention reminder
- **Evening reminder**: Set time for reflection reminder
- **Skip weekends**: Don't send reminders on weekends

### Privacy

- **Send analytics**: Share anonymous usage data
- **Crash reports**: Send crash reports to help improve Dayflow

## Menu bar

Click the Dayflow icon in your menu bar to access:

- **Open Dayflow**: Show main window
- **Start/Stop Recording**: Toggle recording
- **Open Recordings**: View recording files
- **Settings**: Open settings window
- **Check for Updates**: Manually check for updates
- **Quit Dayflow**: Exit the app

## Auto-updates

Dayflow uses Sparkle for automatic updates.

**Update settings**:
- **Auto-check**: Daily check for updates (default: enabled)
- **Auto-download**: Download updates in background (default: enabled)
- **Auto-install**: Install updates on quit (default: disabled)

**To manually check**:
1. Click menu bar icon
2. Select **Check for Updates**

**Update process**:
1. Dayflow checks for updates daily
2. If update available, downloads in background
3. Notifies you when ready to install
4. Install on next quit or manually

## Debug tools

Access debug information from the menu bar.

**Debug menu**:
- **View Logs**: See app logs
- **Test AI Connection**: Verify AI provider setup
- **Clear Cache**: Clear thumbnail cache
- **Reset Database**: Reset timeline database (destructive)
- **Open Data Folder**: View all Dayflow data

<Warning>
  Debug tools are for troubleshooting. Some actions (like Reset Database) are destructive and cannot be undone.
</Warning>
