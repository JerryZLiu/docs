## Get started in three steps

Install Dayflow, grant permissions, and start tracking your day.

### Step 1: Download and install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Dayflow">
    Download the latest `Dayflow.dmg` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/JerryZLiu/Dayflow/releases/latest).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Open the DMG file and drag **Dayflow** into your **Applications** folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch Dayflow">
    Open Dayflow from your Applications folder or Spotlight.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card
  title="Download for Mac"
  icon="download"

  horizontal
>
  Get the latest Dayflow.dmg
</Card>

### Step 2: Grant permissions

Dayflow needs permission to record your screen to create your timeline.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open System Settings">
    When prompted, click **Open System Settings** or navigate manually to:
    
    **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Dayflow">
    Toggle the switch next to **Dayflow** to enable screen recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart if needed">
    You may need to restart Dayflow for the permission to take effect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Dayflow only records your screen locally. No data is sent anywhere without your explicit configuration of an AI provider.
</Note>

### Step 3: Configure AI provider

Choose how you want Dayflow to analyze your screen activity.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Gemini (recommended)">
    **Best for**: Most users who want high-quality analysis
    
    1. Get a free API key from [Google AI Studio](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/api-key)
    2. Open Dayflow settings
    3. Select **Gemini** as your AI provider
    4. Paste your API key
    5. Click **Test Connection**
    
    <Tip>Enable Cloud Billing on your Google Cloud project to prevent Google from training on your data. See [Privacy & data](/privacy) for details.</Tip>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Local models">
    **Best for**: Privacy-focused users who want everything on-device
    
    1. Install [Ollama](https://ollama.com/) or [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/)
    2. Download a vision model (e.g., `llava` for Ollama)
    3. Start the local server
    4. In Dayflow settings, select **Local** as your AI provider
    5. Configure the endpoint URL
    
    <Warning>Local models require more processing power and may drain battery faster.</Warning>
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="ChatGPT/Claude">
    **Best for**: Users who want the highest quality summaries
    
    **Requirements**: Active ChatGPT Plus/Pro or Claude Pro subscription ($20/month)
    
    1. Install [Codex CLI](https://github.com/openai/codex) (ChatGPT) or [Claude Code](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/claude-code)
    2. Sign in with your paid account
    3. In Dayflow settings, select **ChatGPT** or **Claude**
    4. Test the connection
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## You're all set!

Dayflow is now recording your screen and will analyze it every 15 minutes. Your timeline will appear in the main window.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Screen capture is blank or fails">
    Check **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** and ensure **Dayflow** is enabled. You may need to restart the app after granting permission.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="API errors with Gemini">
    Verify your API key is correct and you have network connectivity. Check your API quota at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/).
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Local model is slow or crashes">
    Local models are GPU-intensive. Ensure you're plugged in and have sufficient RAM. Try a smaller model if performance is poor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
